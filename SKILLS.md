# Chicken Farm — Skills update spec: skills that make playing easier

**Goal (the farmer, after Phase 5 and the night fix):** "This phase needs to better tie the skills in the game to making
things easier in the game." He chose: **every branch gets a three-step payoff**, all eleven branches (water and drinkers,
ventilation and heat, feed and silo, chicks, weighing and dead birds first), and **automation that sometimes fails** so the
manual skill still matters. Today 32 of the 33 unlocks (`config.UNLOCKS`) are information views: they help understanding
but save no time, walking or steps. After this update each branch level makes play noticeably easier in the area the branch
is about:

| Step | Name on the tree | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | **See more** | the branch's view (the existing level 1 unlock), easy to find: a key or a button where the job is done, named in the job strip when it helps |
| Level 2 | **Faster** | fewer steps, less walking, no arithmetic, more farm time: the routine job of the branch done in one place or one press |
| Level 3 | **The equipment does it** | a routine job (or one part of it) handed to the farm's equipment, as a real controller does; the job stays on the card as a quick glance; rare faults hand it back |

## 0. Rules that hold everywhere (approved design, do not break)

- **No lessons, no quizzes, no hidden-answer scoring.** A payoff never asks a question. Every new text (the tree's *shows* and
  *saves* lines, the job card's hints, captions, the equipment row, fault lines, settings labels) is checked by test-contracts'
  `VERDICT` words: no "correct", "wrong", "good job", "well done", "mistake", "failed to" …
- **No "correct" / "wrong" messages anywhere.** The equipment reports what it did and what it reads ("Winches: lines 1–4 at
  24 cm, plate 24 cm"), a fault says what stopped and where to look; the farmer reads the gauges, the flock and the records.
- **Every sign fact shows its source.** The new sign lines (§3.12, `content-signs-skills.js`) are all **general practice**:
  nothing about controllers, winches, sight tubes, scales or helpers is on the closed guide lists (PHASE2.md §6.1, PHASE3.md
  §5.1). No page numbers. The guide PDFs and content-map.md are not in this repo.
- **Missing a level's key target fails the level and you replay it.** No target, limit or pass rule changes. A fault never
  silently costs a level (§5.4): it is raised on the flock watch with its cause and place, it stops a Next job skip and shows on
  the night check, it starts in daylight with time to fix it, it is bounded, and a fix by hand always works.
- **Skill tree:** points come from doing a branch's jobs well in play; a milestone is a result, not a question; each branch
  level unlocks a tool or a view — after this update a **payoff** (§3) beside its view. Existing milestones stay (ids, texts,
  rules, thresholds); seven are added (§2.3). **No payoff changes a job's sample size**, so no milestone needs a new rule for it.
- **Days pass in a few minutes of play** with Next job; desktop browser, English, low-poly flat-shaded procedural art, one
  self-contained page (three 0.160.0 from jsDelivr), ARCHITECTURE.md's publishing constraints (no `alert` / `confirm` /
  `prompt`, no `window.open`, no downloads, `localStorage` only inside try/catch, nothing sideways at phone width,
  `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **Earlier phases keep working.** With `config.SKILLS_UPDATE.live` false (main now) nothing a player sees or the model does
  changes: test-sim byte-identical, every node test and steps file as on main. The integrator sets `live` true at the last merge
  (§16); from then on, under automation (a steps file, `navigator.webdriver`) **no payoff acts** until a steps file calls
  `__game.perks.allow(true)` (as the governor and the introduction stay off), so the Phase 1 sandbox, Phase 2's lone brooding
  week, Phase 3's cycle, Phase 4's conditions, Phase 5's panels and the night fix's `test-night` / `req-night` play as before.
  Node tests that build their own world (`runCycle`, `runProfile`, test-level, test-run) make no perks unless they ask for them
  (`runCycle(name, { perks })`, §13.1).

## 1. Player experience walkthrough

### 1.1 The first flock (the tree fills up; level 1 and 2 arrive)
The planning day ends with **Farm Planning level 1** (the plan's own points, 6–7): the caption under the HUD reads *"Farm
Planning 1 · See more: the planning calculator at the office desk. Use it on the planning sheet."* The skill tree's card for
Farm Planning shows three rows, each with what it gives or saves:

> **Level 1 · See more** — Planning calculator at the office desk · *works the sums on the planning sheet*
> **Level 2 · Faster** — Pocket phone · *saves the walk to the office for every order, vet call and booking* · 2 points to go
> **Level 3 · The equipment does it** — The daily record on the phone; the next plan starts from this cycle · *saves the walk
> to the office every evening to sign the day's record* · after a finished flock

At the brooding week's end **Water 2** arrives (*"Water 2 · Faster: read all four sight tubes at the water supply. Use it at the
sight-tube board on the water supply panel. Saves the four one-minute flow tests on flow-test mornings: a cup test only on a
line that reads off."*) and **Chick Arrival & Brooding 2** (*Next chick* and the 2 m reach: it helps on the next arrival day).
On day 8 the job card's *Check drinker flow and height* reads *Water 2: read the sight tubes at the water supply*; the strip
shows each line's water column against the drinker plate's mark for day 8; three lines read at the mark and count as tested;
line 3 reads 2 cm low, so the strip names *line 3: a cup test at its regulator*, and that one flow test finishes the job.

Through weeks 2–3 Heating 2 (*Set to the sign's row* with the row and the day beside it, the pre-heat time on the heat-loss
readout), Moisture 2 (*Fill the timer* in the minimum ventilation calculator, its working printed under it), Lighting 2
(*Program for days 15–35* on the Lights tab), Biosecurity 2 (the tally bucket: every dead bird within 2.5 m in one pick,
counted as it goes in, emptied at the bin after every round as before, with no count there), Feed 2 (the silo gauge's days of
feed and its order key) and, by day 21, Farm Planning 2 (the pocket phone) arrive. Heat & Ventilation 2 comes by day 29 (before the heat wave) and
Growth 2 with the day 28 weigh-in. The job card shows the next payoff of the job in hand: *Weigh sample birds — Growth 2:
weigh the frame in one press · 3 points to go*. By the end of the first flock a careful player has level 2 in every branch
and **no level 3**: level 3 waits for a finished flock (§2.2); the tree says *"13 points: level 3 comes with your next flock"*.

### 1.2 The second flock (the equipment takes jobs over)
The flock is finished: from the next planning day every branch with 13 points is at level 3 (a careful player: all eleven).
The caption at the plan's start lists them once: *"Level 3 now: the equipment does it in 11 branches (Settings → Automation
switches each off)."* From then on *Raise the drinker and feeder lines* reads *drinker lines: handled by the controller
(Water 3) · feeder lines: handled by the controller (Feed 3)* and is done by looking at the heights on the water supply panel
and the rims on the feeder box. The flock watch card gains an **Equipment** row (*"7 on · reading"*). The climate
controller's read shows what each controller did since the last read (*"Timer: stepped to 1 fan 21 s / 300 s at 06:00 for
5,890 birds of 480 g"*), so the morning controller read is also the glance for the timer, the brooders, the stages and the
lights. *Check drinker flow and height* is still the farmer's: the sight tubes, and a cup test on a line that reads off.

On day 24 at 09:12 the line 3 winch motor stalls: the Equipment row turns red, the caption and the notebook say *"Line 3 winch
motor stalled at 31 cm (plate 33 cm) at 09:12; the other lines go on rising. Look at the drinker line 3 regulator: winch it by
hand to the drinker plate's height and reset the motor."* A Next job skip stops there; the job card's drinker part reads
*Line 3: by hand at its regulator*. He walks there, winches it to 33 cm and presses *Reset the motor*: the row is steady again.

### 1.3 Harder flocks
Faults come more often in the harder flocks (§5.2): in the rainy season the controller comes back from a power cut with its fan
stages off until he sets them again at the fan panel (the generator runs only the fans switched on); in the hot season a fan
stage relay trips on a morning and a sensor drifts in the brooding week; a bad batch's chicks meet a drifting sensor or a fouled
scale; the water condition's lines meet a stalled winch. Each is caught by the flock watch (and the night check and the skip's
stops), each names its cause and where to look, each is fixed by doing the job by hand — the skill the first flock taught.

### 1.4 Keeping every job by hand
Settings → **Automation** (§7) switches the equipment off for every branch or one branch at a time: *"Water 3: drinker
winches follow the flock's age"*. With a branch off its jobs are done by hand exactly as before (the level 2 shortcuts stay, as
keys he may use or not). The tree shows *off in Settings* on that level-3 row.

### 1.5 Time saved (what a day costs, §14)
Measured by the play-time model of §14 (the farm day's jobs as the card lists them, walks between their places, panel presses
and waits) and by real input on a second flock's day 16 (§13.3). The bands are set from the model's first measurement (§14.2);
the design estimate is about a sixth less play time on a routine day at level 2 and about a third less at level 3, the rest
going to the day's own problems.

## 2. The skill tree after the update (package "model" for the numbers, "jobs" for the tree)

### 2.1 Three steps per branch
Each branch keeps its three levels and its existing view unlocks (`config.UNLOCKS`, 33 keys) and gains two **payoffs**
(`config.PAYOFFS`, 22 keys): level 2 *Faster*, level 3 *The equipment does it*. Level 1 *See more* is the branch's existing
level 1 view (§3.0 makes each one easy to find). One change of level in the views: **Feed's `siloDays` moves to level 2** (it
is the gauge the level 2 payoff works from) and `feedGraph` to level 3 (each branch still has one view per level;
test-contracts' `1,2,3` holds). The tree's card per branch lists, for each level: the payoff's name, its *saves* line, where it
is used, and the view under it with its own plain words (`config.UNLOCK_SHOWS`, all 33: *also: the last 24 hours of house
temperature and the brooders on the controller*).

### 2.2 Points per flock, the thresholds and the level 3 gate (`config.SKILLS_UPDATE`, switch `live`)
- **A milestone counts in up to two flocks.** Today a milestone's best tier is kept per profile and a second flock adds
  nothing. From `live`: each milestone keeps its best tier **per flock slot** — slot 1 while the profile's `cyclesDone` is 0
  (the first flock), slot 2 once a cycle is finished (`cyclesDone` ≥ 1: the second and every later flock, any condition). A
  branch's points = the sum over both slots of each milestone's tier points. A replay inside the same flock can only raise its
  slot's tier (as now); a third flock raises slot 2 only; a standard start's auto play is not the player's (`level.profile`
  snapshot / restore, as now). A Phase 2–5 profile (format v1, `earned` only) restores into slot 1.
- **Thresholds** `SKILLS_UPDATE.levelPoints` = **[4, 9, 13]** and **the level 3 gate** `SKILLS_UPDATE.level3After` = **1**:
  level 3 needs 13 points over the two slots **and a finished flock** (`profile.cyclesDone ≥ 1`). `config.levelFromPoints(pts,
  cyclesDone)` is the one rule (skills.js `levelOf` uses it; `skills.debug.force` still wins). With `live` false the Phase 5
  thresholds `SKILL_LEVEL_POINTS` [4, 9, 15] hold with no gate. A first flock therefore reaches level 2 at most; a branch with 13
  points at its end is at level 3 from the next plan (`skills.cycleDone` emits EV.SKILL_UNLOCK `{ key, payoff, gate: true }` for
  the payoffs it opens; their caption waits for the next level start, §6.3).
- **Why 13 with the gate, not 15** (the review proposed 15): with 15 the normal farmer (§2.4) reaches level 3 in Brooding,
  Heating, Moisture, Feed and Lighting on day 0–7 of flock 2 — after the arrival helper, the prep-day pre-heat, the prep-day
  lights and the prep-day timer they hand over (`skill-progress --compare 4,9,15`: Brooding 0 of 1, Heating 0 of 1, Lighting 1 of
  2 job days felt). With 13 every normal branch is at level 3 from flock 2's planning day, and every handed-over job of flock 2 is
  the equipment's. The gate, not the points, is what makes level 3 the second flock's.
- `skills.points(branch)` = both slots; `skills.points(branch, { slot: 0 | 1 })` one; `skills.milestones(branch)` gives each
  milestone `{ tier, points, tiers2: [slot1Tier, slot2Tier], points2: [p1, p2] }`; EV.SKILL_POINTS carries `slot`.
- The tree shows each milestone as *2 · 3* (first flock · another flock, the best of each) with the measured detail of the last
  award; the bar's marks at 4 / 9 / 13 of a 26-point scale (clipped at its end); a branch at 13 or more in its first flock shows
  the level 3 pip open with *"comes with your next flock"*.

## 3. The payoffs, branch by branch (`config.PAYOFFS`, `config.SKILL_VIEWS`, `config.UNLOCK_SHOWS`)
Every payoff entry carries `{ branch, level, step: 'faster' | 'auto', name, saves, where, jobs, by }` (+ `system` and `fault`
for an automated one). `saves` is the tree's plain-words line, `where` the unlock caption's "use it at …", `jobs` the job ids the
payoff eases (the job card and the strips read it), `by` the package that builds it. No payoff has a `need`: **no sample size
changes**. **Time saved** figures are design estimates of the §14 model (walking at 4 m/s on the route, 2 s per press, the
panel clock 24 game s per real s); `skill-time.mjs` measures them and the integrator writes the measured figures here. The
tree's `saves` lines promise no figure.

### 4.2 How the controller acts
- **On:** `farm.auto.on[system]` is true only while the level runs (`farm.mode === 'level'`), the payoff holds
  (`perks.auto(system)`: its branch at level 3, `SKILLS_UPDATE.live`, `perks.allowed`, the settings' `automation.all` and
  `automation.<branch>`), its fault is not open (§5) and the power is on (after a cut the system resumes with the power — except
  `stages` after a cut in the rainy season, §5.1). `farm.start` resets `on` to all false (in code); **level.js sets it from the
  perks after every `farm.start`, `farm.enterLevel` and restore (resume, replay, load), and on EV.PERKS / EV.SETTINGS**.
  `runCycle` / `runProfile` set it only with their `perks` option (§13.1).
- **Two ways to act, both in code:** (a) the farm's own actions — `farm.act(id, { ...args, by: 'auto', quiet: true })` for the
  ids farm.act handles (`water.height`, `feed.line`, `vent.timer`, `vent.stages`, `light.program`, `feed.order`); (b) the
  ui-only settings (`heat.setpoint`, `vent.fans`, `vent.pad`: farm.act has no case for them) — farm-auto.js's `farm.act` wrapper
  applies them to env for `by: 'auto'` (`AUTO_ENV_ACTIONS`: `env.setHeaterSetpoint(c)`, `env.setFans(n)`, `env.setPad(on)`).
  Either way: no EV.ACTION (no job counts a controller's step as the farmer's press) and one `EV.AUTO { system, did, text, args,
  day, hour }` per change (a notebook line of kind `auto`, §8).
- **No problem transition is caused by equipment** (the review's issue 3, decided): `farm.js act` skips `actionSymptoms`,
  `cycleSymptoms` and `condSymptoms` for `by: 'auto'` (in code), so a controller's step never finds, acts on or fixes a farm
  problem and never earns a problem milestone (`w3-heat`, `damp-cleared`, `sds-w2` …). A problem job's **state** part may be met
  by the equipment's settings (as by the weather); its act part and the farm's problem record move only on the farmer's own act.
- **The farmer's hand wins for a while** (`AUTO.handHold`, kept in `farm.auto.hold` = `{ winch: { [line]: until }, feed:
  { [line]: until }, timer, brooders, stages, stagesTable, lights }`, env days): a line he winches or a rim he sets holds until the
  next 06:00; his thermostat until the next 06:00; his timer until 18:00 that day (the next 06:00 for one sent after 18:00); his
  fans or pad until 18:00 that day (the next 06:00 for one set after 18:00; only once the stage table is the controller's, from
  day 15 of the flock's age: the review round, so the table never takes his pad back in the afternoon heat); his own stage table until the next 06:00; his light program until the next change of week. The
  panel's line: *"Your setting holds until 18:00, then the controller goes back to the table"*.
- **No auto play of the ideal is changed:** `farm.auto.quietN` > 0 holds every system and every fault. `farm.start` and
  `farm.restore` **keep** it (in code); level.js wraps `debug.goto(…, { auto: true })` in `farm.autoCtl.quiet(true / false)`;
  run.js wraps **the whole `standardStart`** (every goto, auto or not, `run.next()` and the profile's restore) in
  `farm.autoCtl.quiet(true / false)` beside its `level.watch.quiet`. While a standard start plays, no unlock caption or `skill`
  notebook line shows (run.js exposes `run.standardPlaying`; ui.js skips them). `skills.restore` emits EV.SKILL_UNLOCK `{ key:
  null, restore: true }` (in code; not EV.SKILL_POINTS, whose listeners in level.js and records.js would book it as points), so
  the perks refresh and level.js re-syncs `farm.auto.on` after `level.profile.restore(snap)`.
- **Readings:** `farm.autoCtl.status()` → `{ on, systems: { [system]: { state: 'running' | 'held' | 'fault' | 'off', text, since }
  }, faults, last }`; `farm.autoCtl.ok(system, inst, part)` (the reading in the part's band, §4.3); `farm.autoCtl.sensorC()`.

### 4.3 The job handed over: a quick glance (package "jobs": `src/job-variants.js`)
A job of `AUTO_SYSTEMS[system].jobs` made (instance created) while the system is on gets a **variant** rule (the shapes are in
the stub's header). Part `'whole'`: `{ type: 'any', handled: system, of: [GLANCE, original] }`. A named part (`r-raise`'s
`drinkers-raised` for the winch, `pans-raised` for the feed): `lines-raised` splits into `all[drinkers-raised, pans-raised]`
and only that part becomes `any[GLANCE, part]`; the other part stays the farmer's. `GLANCE = all[ live('<keeps>', 'auto-ok',
{ system, part }), lab('Look at <reading> (<place>)', action auto.glance { system }) ]`. The state test `auto-ok` reads
`farm.autoCtl.ok(system, inst, part)`: the system on, no open fault, the reading in band (the winch: every line ±3 cm of the
plate; the timer: 90–130 % of the need; the stages: the stage table of the age running; the brooders: within 1 °C of the row;
the feed: an order of the job's type confirmed or on the way, or > 2 days of feed; the rims ±3 cm; the lights: the program of
the age; the scale: ≥ 300 weighings today). So the job is done by a look at the reading while the equipment keeps it in band —
or, as before, by hand (a fault, a setting turned off, a farmer who wants to). `perks.handled(jobId)` → `[{ system, part, branch,
key, label: 'handled by the controller (Water 3)', reading, glanceAt }]` (in code; `[]` for `r-drinkers`). The card shows one
line per part: *"drinker lines: handled by the controller (Water 3) · look at the heights at the water supply"*.

The variant is taken when the instance is made (the next day's jobs after a level 3 arrives or a setting changes; an open job
keeps its rule, whose manual part still works); `jd.variant = { v, keys, parts }` is saved with the instance.

### 5.2 How often: one rule (`config.FAULT_PLAN`; in code as the stubs' contract)
- **Planned once per flock, at the brooding week's `farm.start`** (farm-auto.js calls `planFaults(farm, env, { flockNo })`;
  level.js passes `skills.profile.cyclesDone`; a later level's `enterLevel` draws nothing) from `createRng(seed ×
  FAULT_PLAN.rngMul + flockNo)` — never `farm.rng`, so the model's own random numbers and every Phase 1–5 trace stay the same —
  into `farm.auto.faults` as items `{ id: null, system: null, phase: 'planned', at }`. **Saved with the farm**, so a replay, a
  load or a resume keeps the same plan.
- **How many:** normal season at most 1 (one item with chance 0.6); hot, rainy, a bad batch and a water problem 2 items.
- **When:** flock days 1–40 (`window.fromDay` / `toDay`), never on a level's last day (`notDays` 7, 14, 21, 35), items at least
  2 days apart (`minGapDays`), between 07:00 and 15:00 (`window.hours`).
- **What:** at an item's time (with `quietN` 0) the fault is picked among the systems **on then** by the plan's weights (hot:
  `stage-trip` and `sensor-drift` twice as likely; rainy: `timer-reset`; a bad batch: `sensor-drift` and `scale-fouled`; water:
  `winch-stall`), each fault's own `hours` / `days` holding (else the next system); none on → `'dropped'`. `order-lost` **arms**
  then and hits the next automatic order within 24 farm hours (none → `'dropped'`).
- **At most one open at a time:** an item due while a fault is open waits for its fix (past day 40 → dropped). An item whose time
  passes while `quietN` > 0 (a standard start's ideal) is dropped. **The rainy season's after-cut trip** (`afterCut:
  'stage-trip'`: EV.POWER on while `stages` is on) is outside the plan: not counted in `max`, exempt from "one open".
- A fault starts inside a Next job skip too: the skip stops on its warning.
- **Tested** (test-auto): over seeds 1–200 the normal season's planned items average 0.5–0.7; the harder flocks plan exactly 2;
  every item inside the window; none on a level's last day; none starts while `quietN` > 0.

### 5.3 Caught by the flock watch, the night check and the skip's stops (model + jobs)
- **The Equipment row** (`watch.status().rows.equipment`, shown while any system is on): green *"4 on · reading"* (the
  systems by name in its detail), amber or red per §5.1 with the fault's own words. A red row (an amber one for the drift while
  the flock is under 7 days) raises one warning (EV.WATCH kind `equipment`): *what* stopped, *the likely cause*, *where to look*
  (its place and plate), with `fix: { text, panel, tab, spot }`, as every warning: a caption, a notebook line, the card's last
  line, a Next job skip's stop.
- **The night check** (`night-check.js`, model; `ui-night.js`, jobs): before a skip through the night every open fault is a row
  of *What is off, and where to set it*, each with *Fix it* (its panel and tab):
  - `timer-reset`: *"The controller restarted on its first timer setting at 14:10: 1 fan 8 s / 300 s against the vent sign's 21 s
    for your birds."* → the controller's Ventilation tab;
  - `stage-trip`: *"The fan stages are off since 08:23; the fans run at 2."* → the fan panel;
  - `sensor-drift`: *"The controller's sensor reads 2.4 °C over the house thermometer: the brooders hold the house 2.4 °C under
    the row tonight."* → the controller's Temperature tab (*Check the sensor*);
  - `order-lost`: *"The feed order of 13:05 is not confirmed: 1.6 days of feed in the silos. Ordered now (after 16:00) the feed
    comes the day after tomorrow at 09:00."* → the pocket phone, else the office phone;
  - `winch-stall`: *"Line 3's winch motor is stalled at 31 cm; the drinker plate says 33 cm tonight and 34 cm tomorrow."* →
    the line 3 regulator.
- **The skip's stops:** a fault's warning is a new flock watch warning, so the skip stops (the night fix's guard). A fault that
  starts while the night is skipped stops it within the farm hour (planned faults start only 07:00–15:00; the after-cut trip can

- **The job card:** a handed-over job whose system has a fault shows its manual part first: *"Line 3: by hand at its regulator
  (the winch motor stalled)"*.

### 5.4 Never silently costing a level
Every fault the controller can know (stall, restart, order, trip) is raised at once on the Equipment row and, red, as a warning;
the drift is raised within 2 farm hours while the flock is under 7 days (amber warns) and within 4 farm hours later (red); the
fouled scale shows on its first weigh-day reads. Each planned fault starts between 07:00 and 15:00 (the stage trip 07:00–10:00),
never on a level's last day; each is bounded (§5.1); each is fixed by the manual job of the branch, which always works (the
variant keeps the original rule). If a level fails with a fault still open, the fail screen's carried line names it (*"the fan
stages were off from 08:23 to 10:23"*). test-auto checks every fault: raised within its bound, caught by the watch (warning and
row), a skip stopped, a night check row, fixed by hand, the level passing with the fix (the ideal with automation, §13.1).

table per rule: each cup test 2 presses, the sight tubes 1, a timer step 4 presses without the calculator and 1 with it, a frame

minute = 2.5 s at 24 game s per real s) + the glance (1 s open) for a handed-over job. The same day three times: no skills,
every branch at level 2, every branch at level 3 (faults off). Printed per job and per day.
